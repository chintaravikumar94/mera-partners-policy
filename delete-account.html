Account & Data Deletion — Mera Partners

Last updated: 27 May 2026  |  Developer: Chinta Ravikumar

This page explains how users of the Mera Partners Android app can request deletion of their account and associated data, in compliance with Google Play's User Data policy.

1. What data will be deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data associated with your Mera Partners account will be permanently removed from our Firebase servers:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your Google Sign-In linkage (if used)
  • Your profile picture and uploaded images
  • Your role data (Partner / Admin) and assigned permissions
  • Your SKU requests, sell-product entries, and sell-digital-service entries
  • Your push-notification token (FCM)
  • Your in-app activity history and preferences

2. What data may be retained

Some data may be retained for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons for up to 90 days, after which it is permanently deleted:

  • Anonymized transactional records (with personal identifiers removed)
  • System logs required for security audits
  • Aggregate analytics data that does not identify you personally

3. How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Mera Partners account and all associated data, send an email from the email address registered to your account, with the following details:

Email to: chintaravikumar1994@gmail.com

Subject: Mera Partners — Account Deletion Request

Body should include:
  1. Your registered name
  2. Your registered email address
  3. Your registered phone number (if any)
  4. The reason for deletion (optional)
Important: The email must come from the same address registered to your Mera Partners account. This is required to verify your identity before we delete your data.

4. Timeline

  1. Acknowledgement: Within 3 business days of receiving your request.
  2. Verification: We will verify your identity by matching the request email with our records.
  3. Deletion: Your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days of verified request.
  4. Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

5. After deletion

Once your account is deleted:

  • You will no longer be able to log into the Mera Partners app with that account.
  • You can create a new account at any time, but previous data cannot be recovered.
  • Any partner referrals, ratings, or assigned tasks will be anonymized.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about the deletion process or data retention policy, contact us at chintaravikumar1994@gmail.com.

See also our Privacy Policy for full details on how Mera Partners handles your data.
