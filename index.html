Mera Partners — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 May 2026

Information We Collect

We collect your name, email, mobile number, profile photo, and customer details you enter while using the app. Financial data (order amounts, commissions) is stored for your dashboard.

How We Use Your Data

To operate your partner account, process service orders, calculate commissions, and provide support.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud).

Data Security

All network traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Firestore Security Rules restrict access so you can only see your own data.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account anytime from the More tab. All your personal data is removed within 30 days.
